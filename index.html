03 / THE IDEA

Not Cajun ramen.
Cajun + ramen.

Shaolin Slurp keeps the precision of a ramen shop and lets Louisiana pantry staples speak in their own voice: roux, smoked sausage, okra, crawfish, corn and pepper heat.

The design follows the same rule. Japanese utility, New Orleans poster energy, food-truck immediacy. No lantern-and-fleur-de-lis costume party.
